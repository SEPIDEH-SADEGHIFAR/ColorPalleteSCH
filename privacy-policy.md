Privacy Policy

This app, AWBY, is developed by Seyedeh Sepideh Sadeghi Far.

The app does not create user accounts, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not display third‑party advertising.

Data we access

Photos: If you choose to import an image, the app reads that image locally to extract colors and build palettes. Images are not uploaded to any server and are not shared by the app.

App data: Palettes, colors and settings you create are stored locally on your device using Apple’s storage frameworks.

On‑device AI
Some features use Apple Intelligence / on‑device machine learning provided by Apple to suggest color palettes. These features run on your device using Apple’s system frameworks; the app does not send your palettes or images to any external server.

Sharing
If you export or share images or color cards, they are passed through the system share sheet to apps you choose (such as saving to Photos or sending in a message). What happens after sharing is governed by those apps’ own privacy policies.

Third‑party services
This app does not integrate third‑party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or social login services.
